FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to have Canva?

No. A free Canva account lets you edit the files later, but I can also send you finished images and PDFs.

Do you post for me?

I can. Scheduling is billed at the hourly rate, or you can post the ready-made files yourself.

What if I don't have good photos?

We can use your phone photos, free stock images, or designs that rely on type and colour. I'll suggest what works best.

Is there a contract?

There's no long-term commitment. I'll send a simple written agreement before we start so we're both clear on scope and price.

How do I pay?

Projects are 50% up front and 50% on delivery. Hourly work is invoiced at the end of each month.
